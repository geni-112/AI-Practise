# Government and public-sector pack

Track policy, mandate, budget, procurement, leadership, legislation, public services, shared platforms, data strategy, AI, cloud, digital identity, interoperability, cybersecurity, sovereignty, accessibility, and regional or agency coordination.

Treat agencies and programs—not "the government"—as distinct entities. Verify jurisdiction, legal authority, fiscal year, funding status, procurement stage, implementation owner, and delivery partner.

Ask which public outcome and user group are targeted; whether the item is policy, funded program, tender, award, pilot, or deployed service; what privacy, residency, accessibility, security, interoperability, and procurement controls apply; and what engagement is permissible. Never infer an award from a framework agreement or market consultation.
