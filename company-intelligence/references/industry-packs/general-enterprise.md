# General enterprise pack

Use the universal enterprise framework without adding a specialist industry taxonomy. Emphasize the dimensions most material to the company's business model and the user's intent.

Track strategy, latest financials, executive statements, growing and declining segments, customers, products, geographic expansion, investment, capacity, operations, supply chain, restructuring, acquisitions, partnerships, leadership, workforce, regulation, competitors, risks, and—when relevant—digital, data, AI, cloud, cybersecurity, and major IT programs.

Select KPIs that reflect the business model; define them and avoid cross-company comparisons when accounting or operating definitions differ.

Ask: What changed versus prior commitments or expectations? Which growth, cost, risk, customer, workforce, capability, or operating-model priority does it reveal? Which stakeholder owns the outcome? If a technology implication exists, what capability is necessary rather than merely adjacent? What question would confirm need, urgency, ownership, success metric, constraints, and disqualifying evidence?
