# Retail and consumer pack

Track same-store and online growth, traffic, basket/order value, margin, inventory, fulfillment, loyalty, store footprint, regional expansion, brands/categories, pricing, advertising, marketplace, payments, supply chain, acquisitions, partnerships, and leadership.

Track technology across commerce, POS, ERP, CRM/CDP, personalization, retail media, forecasting, warehouse/transport, computer vision, data/AI, cloud, cybersecurity, and omnichannel operations.

Ask which customer journey, category, geography, or supply-chain constraint changed; whether it targets growth, margin, availability, or loyalty; what data and integration are required; and which commercial, operations, supply chain, marketing, or technology leader owns the outcome.
