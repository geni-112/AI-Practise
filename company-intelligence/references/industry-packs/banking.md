# Banking pack

Track strategy: digitization, AI, cloud, internationalization, efficiency, regulation, risk, and capital allocation. Track businesses: retail, corporate, cards, wealth, investment banking, payments, and insurance where relevant.

Use relevant KPIs: revenue, net income, ROE/ROTE, net interest margin, efficiency or cost-to-income, CET1/capital, deposits, loans, customers, digital adoption, NPL/NPE, provisions, payment volume, and assets under management. Preserve each bank's definitions and reporting currency.

Track technology: core banking, payments, channels, data, AI, cloud, cybersecurity, fraud/AML, developer platforms, open banking, resilience, and major procurement. Track acquisitions, partnerships, leadership and regulator actions.

Ask: Which business line or geography drives the change? Is management prioritizing growth, efficiency, risk, or compliance? What customer journey or operating process is affected? What modernization dependency, control requirement, or data constraint follows? Which CIO/CDO/CISO/COO/business stakeholder should validate it?

Search local regulator filings, investor materials, earnings calls, procurement notices, partner announcements, and terms such as transformation, core modernization, digital users, branch strategy, AI, cloud, data platform, fraud, AML, cybersecurity, resilience, and open finance.
